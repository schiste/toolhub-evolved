# Technical and Marketing Notes

- The connection budget test asserted the fleet beside five job processes at a single unit each -- 8 + 10 = 18 of 20 -- and passed, while production returned a `max_user_connections` 500 at 14:45:50 on 2026-09-02. Both were true. `projection_refresh` declares two concurrent units through `job_runner.configure(concurrency=...)` and so costs four connections where every other job costs two, and the test hard-coded one unit per job, which meant it could not see the single exception the `concurrency` parameter exists to express. The real total with that job running was 20 of 20. A budget test that does not read the declared widths is not testing the budget.
- Spending exactly the grant is not the same as fitting inside it, and the distinction is the whole failure mode. `pool_recycle` can hold an old and a new connection together for an instant and `retry_on_disconnect` disposes a pool and reconnects mid-run; at 20 of 20 there is nowhere to put either, which is why this surfaced intermittently under transient load rather than as a steady overspend. The observed 500 landed inside the window where the deploy's own `projection-refresh` overlapped the once-a-minute jobs, and stopped when that job finished: 830 requests afterwards produced no 500s, no `max_user_connections`, and no `QueuePool` timeouts.
- `PARALLEL_SYNC_WORKERS` is now 1, so the two account generations run in turn and the job costs two connections like the rest. It runs every six hours and both generations are network-bound, so the wall clock absorbs it. The executor stays, because the width is a budget decision rather than a structural one: if the grant grows, that constant is the only thing that changes. `account_demand()` replaces the hand-summed arithmetic -- fleet, plus the widest job that declares concurrency, plus the rest at a single unit -- and the invariant now requires `ACCOUNT_HEADROOM` to be left unspent. It reads the width from `projection_refresh.PARALLEL_SYNC_WORKERS`, so raising it back to two fails the test rather than production: at two the plan is exactly 20 and is rejected for leaving nothing over, at three it is 22 and rejected outright, at one it is 18.
- Validation: 3,604 proxy tests pass with 25 skipped, `ruff check`, `ruff format` and cspell clean, and the committed Aethyme graph index was regenerated over the changed files with the pinned 0.4.2 pair and the indexer built from revision `d3af4226`. The regression guard was first written asserting that the old setting exceeded the limit; it failed on its first run because the old setting equals the limit, and that correction -- from "over the line" to "spends everything" -- is what the fix is actually about.
