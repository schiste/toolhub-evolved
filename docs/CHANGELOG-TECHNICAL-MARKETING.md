# Technical and Marketing Notes

- A job process was budgeted two connections, reasoning that a unit of work holds an advisory lock and a session at once. `run_job` holds more. When a caller queues for its lock it takes an _intent_ lock for the whole run alongside the real one, both on one `ExitStack`, so `people_reconcile` runs a session underneath two held locks and needs three. Its pool topped out at two: `QueuePool limit of size 1 overflow 1 reached, connection timed out, timeout 10.00`. Seven components hit it -- `people-identity-reconcile`, `people-attribution-reconverge` and `phabricator-realname-sync` repeatedly, plus single occurrences in `catalog-sync`, `projection-refresh`, `source-attestations` and the deploy's own projection refresh. Three reached the guard's three-failure breaker and were disabled; all have been reset.
- `CONNECTIONS_PER_LOCKING_UNIT` is three, and `run_job` declares whether it locks, because `run_job` takes the lock and is the only place that can say so without every entrypoint repeating it. `projection_refresh` declares it too, its phases taking advisory locks outside `run_job`. Three is the measured width rather than a derived one: `people_reconcile`'s third connection is traceable, but `phabricator-realname-sync` does not queue for its lock and the path that spends its third has not been identified. `CONCURRENT_JOB_PROCESSES` drops from the measured five to a planned four, because counting a locking job honestly leaves five at 21 of a 20-connection grant. Demand is 17 of 20 with two held back.
- The webservice side of the same budget is holding without qualification: 15,369 requests since the previous deploy with zero 500s and zero `max_user_connections`, against 226 connection failures and 107 500s on the day it was found. Every remaining failure was on the job side.
- `npm audit` began reporting seven advisories in transitive dev dependencies, blocking the JS quality gate on every branch -- `main` had passed on the same tree forty minutes earlier. `npm audit fix` was a no-op because the parents pin ranges excluding the patched versions. Two were lockfile drift rather than installed reality: `npm audit` reads `package-lock.json`, which pinned `nanoid` 3.3.16 while 3.3.18 was installed. The existing `typed-rest-client` override pinned `qs` to an exact `6.15.3`, which the widened advisory now covers -- an exact pin cannot self-correct when a range grows underneath it, where a caret would have. Overrides now force `qs` `^6.16.0`, `nanoid` `^3.3.18` and `js-yaml` `^5.4.1`.
- `js-yaml` is the one carrying risk: a major bump forced on `stylelint`'s `cosmiconfig`, which expects 4.x, with no in-range option because the advisory records that the 4.x fix was not backported. It is verified rather than assumed -- `stylelint` runs clean, as do `eslint --max-warnings 0`, `prettier --check`, `tools/checks.mjs` and 1,387 unit tests across 89 files. `npm audit --audit-level=moderate` reports 0 vulnerabilities, down from 7.
